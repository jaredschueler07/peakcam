# P1 coverage import, batch 3: Canada and the Andes (2026-10-20)
> Block P1 of `docs/research/ai-assistant-first/2026-10-18-parity-plan.md`, the Canada and Argentina/Chile rows of the
> competitor's lead list (47 + 4; one "Canadian" row, Bigrock Mountain, is in Maine). The lead list named the resorts;
> every value below was read on the operator's own site or an official source (the pass operators' resort lists,
> Wikidata for Q-ids, a provincial or municipal office for the Andes operating status), and every cam embed was verified
> the way the P8 gap audit did (`docs/cams/gap-audit-2026-10-18.md`, section 1): YouTube oEmbed 200; image GET 200
> `image/*` with no Referer and no cache-buster; iframe 200 with no `X-Frame-Options` and no restrictive
> `frame-ancestors`. Verification timestamps are 2026-10-20 15:0x to 15:3x UTC. Rows live in `data/resorts.csv`,
> `data/cams.csv`, `data/resort-profiles.csv`, `data/resort-openings.csv`.
>
> **Units.** Canadian and Andean operators publish metres, hectares and centimetres; the CSVs carry feet, acres and
> inches (m x 3.28084, ha x 2.47105, cm / 2.54), and each profile row's notes say which numbers were converted and
> which were derived (a base that is summit minus the operator's vertical, for instance).
>
> **Station rule.** `snotel_station_id` stays blank on every row: NRCS has no stations in Canada or the Andes (plan
> section 2, P1 and P3). Canadian rows get their measured numbers from the P3 station layer (`stations-sync`: GHCN-Daily
> snow reporters, BC ASWS pillows for BC, ECCC SWOB hourly stations) and their snow report from `model-sync`
> (Open-Meteo); the Andes rows are Open-Meteo only. Every resort that ships with no cam is listed with its nearest P3
> station in `tests/resort-catalog.test.ts` so the gap is a decision.
>
> Import on the mini: `npm run import-resorts:standalone` then `npx tsx scripts/import-profiles.ts` and
> `node scripts/seed-resort-openings.mjs`; a deploy must follow before `/resorts/{slug}` exists
> (`dynamicParams = false`).
