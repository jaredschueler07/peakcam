## I. About page (narrative)

**Lead**  
Every mountain. Every cam. One glance.

**Body (direction)**  
PeakCam is the fastest way to check real-time conditions across 150+ ski resorts from the Rockies to the Andes. Live webcams, snow totals, forecasts, trail counts — one page, free, no account required.

Winter doesn’t clock out. When North America melts out, the Andes light up — PeakCam covers both hemispheres so there’s always a season live somewhere.

Built for the Thursday-night decision: which mountain actually got it. No tab-juggling resort sites and dead cam aggregators.

## L. Voice checklist for new strings

- [ ] Says what PeakCam *does* or what the user *gets*  
- [ ] No “goods,” “spinning,” “send it,” “shred the gnar”  
- [ ] Could sit next to mono data readouts without irony  
- [ ] Works with Fraunces italic accent on one word max  
- [ ] Survives dual-hemisphere coverage (not “North America only”)
