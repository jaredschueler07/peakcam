# 00 — Brand brief

## Aesthetic first (locked)

**This brief is about words and positioning.** Visual direction is fixed:

> Rugged **REI / outdoor-catalog** feel + existing **ski-poster UI** (cream, forest, bark, stamps).

See `00-aesthetic-lock.md`. Do not “refresh” PeakCam into luxury travel, minimal SaaS, or bro-Instagram while fixing taglines.

---

## Product (what PeakCam is)

PeakCam is a free site for **live mountain webcams + snow reports + conditions** across many ski resorts. Users compare mountains in one glance: base depth, 24h/48h snow, ratings, cams, map.

Stack/context: Next.js app at peakcam.io. ~150+ resorts after South America expansion (Chile + Argentina), not NA-only.

**Primary JTBD**

1. Thursday night: *Is it worth going / which mountain?*  
2. Storm cycle: *Who got the snow?*  
3. Morning of: *What does the cam actually show?*  
4. Off-season NA: *Where is winter live?* (Andes)

## Why the old lines fail

| Line | Problem |
|------|---------|
| “Got the goods?” | Resort social slang (e.g. Whistler `#GetTheGoods`). Vague. Not product. |
| “The lift’s spinning somewhere” | Fortune-cookie ski wisdom. Passive. Could be any mountain IG account. |
| “No marketing noise” alone | Strong *position*, weak as the only hero — doesn’t say what the product *is*. |

**Visual / voice mismatch historically:**  
Visual system = rugged REI + 70s/80s ski poster (cream, forest, stamp) — **this stays.**  
Voice tried = crew-chat bro slang — **this changes.**

**New pairing**

- Visual: **unchanged** — REI rugged, warm film, poster stamps, topo paper  
- Voice: plainspoken tool that doesn’t oversell (no “got the goods”)  

---

## Voice principles

### Do

1. Lead with product: cams, depth, fresh, open trails.  
2. Prefer questions users already ask (“Where’s it good?”).  
3. Anti-hype as discipline, not swagger.  
4. Short, concrete, zero metaphor stacking.  
5. Ski jargon only when operational: *pow day*, *base*, *24h* = labels.

### Don’t

1. Resort hashtag cosplay.  
2. Fortune-cookie ski wisdom.  
3. Sound like a local guide’s Instagram captions.  
4. Lead with attitude before function.  
5. Invent new colors/fonts outside the design system.

### Tone dial

| Too cold | Sweet spot | Too hot |
|----------|------------|---------|
| “Access multi-resort telemetry” | “Check conditions before you go” | “Got the goods, send it fam” |
| NOAA PDF | Lodge poster + clean board | Epic Pass ad |

## Dual-hemisphere story (SA launch)

Factual, not personality:

- Coverage spans Rockies → Andes.  
- Winter is always live somewhere.  
- Homepage can signal Chile & Argentina when data is live (existing UI: Southern Hemisphere banner + state chips).

Seasonal line is secondary to the core tool promise.

---

## Brand promise tests

A good PeakCam line should pass:

1. **Product test** — Does a first-time visitor know this is cams + snow reports?  
2. **Anti-hype test** — Would OpenSnow users who hate “overexuberant” forecasts still trust us?  
3. **Poster test** — Does it sit well in Fraunces italic under the wordmark?  
4. **Not-a-resort test** — Could Whistler or Vail say the exact same thing as brand? If yes, rewrite.

**Decision tool + anti-hype.**

- Hero: **Where’s it good?**  
- Sub: Live webcams and real snow reports. Pick a mountain before you leave.  
- Featured: **Best right now.** / **Looking solid.**  
- Footer: **Check before you go.**  
- Auth: **Save your mountains.**  
- CTA: **See conditions →**
