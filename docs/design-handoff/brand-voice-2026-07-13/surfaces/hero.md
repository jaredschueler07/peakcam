# Surface — Homepage hero

**Live component:** `components/home/PeakHero.tsx`  
**Layout:** Full viewport (`h-screen`), topo paper bg currently; photo hero optional.

**Aesthetic:** Rugged REI + ski-poster (see `00-aesthetic-lock.md`). Do not restyle into luxury travel hero.

---

## Job

First 3 seconds:  
1. Brand recognition (PeakCam wordmark)  
2. What it is (cams + snow)  
3. Emotional/functional hook without ski-bro cosplay  

---

## ★ Recommended copy

```
[eyebrow]  EST. 2025 · {N} RESORTS
[wordmark] PeakCam
[tagline]  Where’s it good?
[body]     Live webcams and real snow reports from the Rockies
           to the Andes. Pick a mountain before you leave.
[cta]      See conditions →
```

### Type

- Eyebrow: JetBrains Mono, uppercase, tracking wide, ink-on-cream or cream-on-ink pill  
- Wordmark: Fraunces black; “Cam” italic alpenglow `#d9552f`  
- Tagline: Fraunces italic bold, bark-dk or ink  
- Body: DM Sans medium, bark  
- CTA: pill, alpenglow fill, ink border, stamp shadow

### Layout notes

- Prefer **type over calm sky or soft gradient**, not over the brightest peak highlight.  
- Current production uses **topo cream paper CSS**, not a full-bleed photo — that paper/topo look is on-brand; don’t abandon it for glossy full-bleed only.  
- If using photo: warm scrim, film grain, stamp CTA — not Apple-style blur glass.  
- Keep nav readable (ink links on cream / or cream on dark gradient).  
- Leave room for mobile wordmark scale (currently very large display type).

---

## Explore / don’t explore

| Explore | Don’t |
|---------|-------|
| Tagline length 2–4 words | “Got the goods?” |
| Question form | “Lift’s spinning…” |
| Sub that names product | Sub that is only attitude |

---

## Acceptance for this surface

- First-time user can answer “what is this site?” after reading body.  
- Tagline works in Fraunces italic without looking like a resort ad.  
- SA coverage is mentionable in body without dominating.
