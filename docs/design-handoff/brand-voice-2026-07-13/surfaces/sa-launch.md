# Surface — South America launch

**Aesthetic lock:** SA is a *geography* expansion, not a visual rebrand.  
Still rugged REI + poster UI — not “luxury Andes ski vacation” marketing.  
See `00-aesthetic-lock.md`.

## Product facts for designers

- ~20 resorts Chile + Argentina shipping  
- `state` values: full names `Chile` / `Argentina` (filter chips work as-is)  
- Hero SA resorts for popular sort: Portillo, Valle Nevado, Cerro Catedral, Las Leñas  
- Data parity with NA once model-sync lands (not a designer task)

## Copy for SA moments

### Homepage body (can stay year-round)

Live webcams and real snow reports from the Rockies to the Andes.

### Seasonal banner (when SA data exists)

```
Southern Hemisphere · in season now
Chile & Argentina are live — {N} Andes resorts with cams and snow reports.
```

### Optional campaign line

```
Winter’s always on.
```

Use sparingly — seasonal campaign, not the forever product tagline.

## Don’t

- Don’t invent Spanish UI / full i18n for this pass (explicit non-goal in design doc).  
- Don’t use bright flag colors outside palette — chips stay ink/cream system.  
- Don’t show generic Swiss Alps and call it Andes. Prefer assets 03 / 10 / 12.
