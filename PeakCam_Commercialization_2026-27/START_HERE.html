PeakCam Commercialization Portfolio — Start Here

Prepared for Jared Schueler | September 9, 2026 | 2026–27 US ski season

The recommendation

Build a trustworthy Colorado camera utility first. Fix the two placeholder entries, document source/image rights, prove repeat pre-drive use, and test approved referrals plus small measured local partnerships. Do not spend the season building a national forecast platform before proving this narrower job.

Open these files in order

  1. PeakCam_Commercialization_Handbook.html — the 21-section business/administrative portfolio, readable offline in a browser. The Markdown version is LLM-friendly; Chapters/ contains each of the 21 deliverables separately.
  2. PeakCam_Financial_and_Operating_Model.xlsx — editable assumptions, three 24-month scenarios, 6/12/24-month summaries, five traffic stages, 36-action roadmap, marketing budgets, 28 risks, 15 experiments and 29 event specifications. Formula-driven, with source notes and explicit hypothetical inputs.
  3. PeakCam_Prospect_CRM.xlsx — 100 researched organizations, First 25, First 20 resort targets, personalized drafts, follow-up dates, stages and a pipeline dashboard. Filter by priority and verify a source before sending.
  4. PeakCam_Outreach_Playbook.html — all First 25 briefs, seven full sequences, eight sales materials, deal proposals and the weekly founder sales routine. Markdown is provided for editing and LLM use.
  5. Sources_and_Evidence.html — core code/research sources and organization-level source/verification notes.

Spreadsheet conventions

Blue inputs are editable; black cells contain local formulas and green formulas link to other sheets. USD values use dashes for zero and parentheses for negatives. The model's monthly figures are scenarios, not actuals. Pay attention to affiliate/ad settlement lag, founder-time sensitivity and the separate cash funding requirement.

The workbook's Scenario Summary is the authoritative live calculation after edits; CSV/JSON and written tables are the original September 9 snapshot and do not automatically update when you edit Excel. Roadmap costs are planning allowances, not invoices or an additional budget to blindly add to the financial case. Marketing tiers are total seasonal envelopes, not monthly commitments.

Your first workday

Repair the placeholder Breckenridge embed records and verify the remaining official links. Recheck the five first resort contacts: Loveland, Monarch, Ski Cooper, Sunlight and Granby. Use the permission-first drafts, not an unearned advertising pitch. Begin the Roundshot/HDOnTap/Panomax licensing inquiries in parallel. Do not send images or imply partnerships without the relevant permission.

Important evidence limits

The connected inspection established an early static Next.js directory, three resorts/five entries, source rendering modes and a production-ready Vercel deployment. It did not establish actual traffic, income, bills, complete policy adequacy, a comprehensive open-issue audit, executed tests or interactive mobile/live-playback performance. Earlier broad feature plans are not assumed present.

No professional trademark clearance, individualized state-law/tax opinion, source licenses or signed partner agreements were obtained. Some CRM routes are general service/media inboxes or public directory listings rather than confirmed buyers. Missing LinkedIn/person/email fields are N/A, not guessed. Research did not send any email, message or application, change code, deploy the site or purchase a service.

Model interpretation

The base scenario assumes substantial growth and still produces only about $4,500 earned revenue in the first 12 months, with roughly $4,400 peak funding need. It excludes founder pay and tax from the operating result; an explicit founder-time sensitivity shows the economic cost of unpaid work. Do not commit capital based on the breakout scenario as a prediction.

Maintenance

Reverify contacts before outreach, prices before purchase, laws before implementing a covered feature and camera sources before promoting them. Update observed baseline metrics first. Mark each task with actual completion evidence; all tasks initially start Not started. Keep the source/permission register separate from your prospect pipeline, and keep research contacts separate from an opted-in newsletter.
