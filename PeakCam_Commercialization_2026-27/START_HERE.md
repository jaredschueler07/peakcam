## Spreadsheet conventions
Blue inputs are editable; black cells contain local formulas and green formulas link to other sheets. USD values use dashes for zero and parentheses for negatives. The model's monthly figures are scenarios, not actuals. Pay attention to affiliate/ad settlement lag, founder-time sensitivity and the separate cash funding requirement.

The workbook's Scenario Summary is the authoritative live calculation after edits; CSV/JSON and written tables are the original September 9 snapshot and do not automatically update when you edit Excel. Roadmap costs are planning allowances, not invoices or an additional budget to blindly add to the financial case. Marketing tiers are total seasonal envelopes, not monthly commitments.

## Model interpretation
The base scenario assumes substantial growth and still produces only about $4,500 earned revenue in the first 12 months, with roughly $4,400 peak funding need. It excludes founder pay and tax from the operating result; an explicit founder-time sensitivity shows the economic cost of unpaid work. Do not commit capital based on the breakout scenario as a prediction.
