# First-wave outreach drafts

Prepared September 13, 2026. **Drafts only; nothing sent.** These replace the obsolete first-wave wording. They describe current configuration and request the precise rights needed; they do not claim audience size, source clearance or existing partnerships.

Before sending, Jared supplies the actual sender mailbox/business postal address, checks suppression and approves the recipients and final text. Verify the live page and actual displayed source one last time. A retrieved contact page establishes a public route, not delivery or licensing authority. Never put the prospect list into an opted-in newsletter.

For commercial messages, accurate sender identity, a valid postal address and a working opt-out belong in the final message. Maintain one suppression record across channels. [FTC business guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business)

Each draft below uses a common signature; replace both placeholders before sending:

> Jared Schueler  
> PeakCam | [BUSINESS EMAIL] | [BUSINESS POSTAL ADDRESS]  
> If you prefer no further partnership messages, reply and I’ll stop.

The exact-source notes are for scope review. Include only relevant URLs in the final email or accompanying plain-text outline; do not attach camera imagery without the relevant use rights. Proposed source permission is separate from buying an ad, granting all marketing rights, or accepting an affiliate application.

## Follow-up and evidence

After an authorized send, record actual send time and schedule one useful follow-up around seven days later. Do not calculate follow-ups from the original September 10–18 planning dates. Stop on refusal/opt-out and share the suppression state across channels. The default here is one initial message plus one follow-up; further contact requires a new reason and recipient context.

Follow-up draft:

> Hi [team/name] — following up on the specific camera-source and permission question below. Is there a better person or published process for this request? We can limit the scope to an attributed official link if that is your preferred route. Thanks for pointing me in the right direction. [Common signature]

A useful reply records the responsible person, authority for each use, exact cameras/URLs, domains/territories, credit, expiry, fees, revocation and private evidence reference. A general “sounds good” or supplier quote does not complete all rights fields. Separate permission, distribution, affiliate approval and paid sponsor stages in the queue.
